Privacy Policy

Universal Scoreboard · Last updated September 6, 2026

Summary

Universal Scoreboard does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no network connection of any kind.

What the app stores

Any information you enter — team names, scores, colors, custom logos, or custom sound files — is saved only on your own Mac, in the app's local settings and sandboxed storage. This data never leaves your device, is never sent to us or any third party, and we have no access to it.

Third-party services

Universal Scoreboard does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no data of any kind, it does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version adds an optional online feature — we will update this page and revise the date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to myworlddiscovered@gmail.com.
