Frequently asked questions

  • How do I show the scoreboard on a projector or second screen?
    Open the Control Panel, then use Open Score Banner Window or Open Projector Window and drag it to your external display.
  • Can I customize team names, colors, and logos?
    Yes — open Settings from the Control Panel to set team names, colors, fonts, and upload custom logos or images.
  • Can I use my own buzzer or alert sound?
    Yes — in Sound Alerts settings, choose Upload a custom sound file to replace any of the built-in buzzers.
  • Which sports are supported?
    Basketball, volleyball, soccer, and badminton (including multi-court setups), with more planned.
  • How do I reset my settings?
    In Settings, choose Reset All Settings to restore defaults for the current sport, or Reset Everything to start fresh.
